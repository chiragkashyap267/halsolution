About Us ♻️

Your trusted partner for hassle-free scrap collection and sustainable recycling solutions. 🌍

Who We Are 👋

Hal Solution Private Limited is an electronics recycling company based in Site V, Greater Noida, Uttar Pradesh. We help households, businesses and organizations manage end-of-life electronics through collection, recycling and refurbishment.

Whether it's your old newspapers 📰, electronics 💻, metals ⚙️, or plastics 🥤 — we ensure everything gets responsibly recycled and never ends up in landfills.

Our Mission 🎯

To simplify waste disposal through innovation, ensuring fair returns and a greener planet 🌱 for future generations.
